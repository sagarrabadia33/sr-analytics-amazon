The problem

Amazon gives you data. It doesn't give you answers.

Between Seller Central reports, settlement files, and spreadsheets, the numbers are everywhere — and rarely agree with each other.

✕ Scattered

Reports live in a dozen places

Orders in one tab, fees in a settlement file, inventory in another export. Stitching them together by hand eats hours every week.

✕ Manual

Spreadsheets that break

Copy-paste pipelines go stale the moment something changes, and one wrong cell quietly throws off every number downstream.

✕ Unclear

No true view of profit

Top-line sales look great until fees, refunds, and reimbursements are accounted for. Most sellers never see the real margin.

Security & trust

Your data, handled the way it should be

We follow the Amazon Selling Partner API Data Protection Policy and collect only what's needed to provide your reporting.

🔒 You stay in control

Access is granted through Amazon's authorization flow and can be revoked by you at any time from Seller or Vendor Central.

🛡️ No buyer PII

We don't request or store buyer names, addresses, or contact details. Our reporting runs on business-level data.

🔐 Encrypted & least-privilege

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and internal access is restricted to only what's required.

🧭 Used only for you

We never sell your data or use it for advertising. It's used solely to deliver the reporting you authorized.
